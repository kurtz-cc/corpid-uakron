System Definition

Phase: Define Weight: 10% Due: Wednesday, October 14

Your logo becomes a system. For each of your two directions, you'll build a Quick Reference Style Guide: the logo and its lockups, color, type, and a style-scape that shows it all working together.

Before You Start

  • Get my sign-off on your logo first.No style-scapes until I've signed off on your logo, for each direction. Your logo should be as good as it can possibly be before you build on it. Changes later are still possible, just a lot harder.
  • How sign-off works.Show me in class during studio time or Wednesday's check-in. Missed me? Email it to jkurtz@uakron.edu by Sunday, Oct 4. Goal: everyone signed off by Monday, Oct 5. Each direction is signed off on its own, so you can start one style-scape while you refine the other.
  • Ready for sign-off?Holds up at half an inch. Works in black only, and reversed. Clean construction: few anchor points, no Image Trace. Type kerned by eye. Compared against your sketch: it kept what made the sketch good. Pushed through several rounds, not your first digital version.
  • Two directions.Two businesses (one concept each), or two concepts from one business. Both carry through all of Define. You narrow to one on Monday, Oct 19.

The Assignment

  • One Quick Reference Style Guide per direction.Two pages each, 16:9. Two guides total.
  • Page 1: logos, typography, color.Primary and secondary lockups, icon only, and reversed versions. Each typeface and weight named (e.g. “Roboto Bold,” not “bold”), with an A to Z specimen. Each color with HEX, RGB, CMYK, and Pantone values.
  • Page 2: style-scape.One designed board showing how the brand looks and feels: your logo, a headline and tagline in your typefaces, a short paragraph in the brand's voice (no lorem ipsum), your color palette, an imagery direction, and supporting graphic elements (patterns, shapes, textures).
  • Written rationale.One for each direction: why these choices, tied to your research.
  • Turn in one PDF in Brightspace.Both guides and both rationales, before class on Wednesday, Oct 14.

How It's Graded

50 points. Each line is yes or no. Both guides are graded together.

  • Process (10).Explored several style-scape directions first (5). Kept refining the mark after digitizing (5).
  • Logos & Lockups (12).Primary lockup refined, production-ready (4). Secondary lockups: alternate, icon only (4). Works reversed and in one color (4).
  • Color & Type (12).3 to 6 colors, each with a clear role (4). Complete values: HEX, RGB, CMYK, Pantone (4). Exact weights named, clear hierarchy (4).
  • Style-Scape (10).Reads as one brand, true to your research (5). Built from your system: logo, type, color (5).
  • Rationale & Professionalism (6).Rationale explains why, tied to research (3). On time, clean files, proper naming (3).
