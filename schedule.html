Fall 2026 Schedule

This is the schedule we will follow this semester, but expect it to change and adjust as we go. We'll keep this page up to date, so check back often. If the syllabus shows a different schedule, this page is correct, not that one.

Regular meeting time: M/W 5:15 to 8:00pm, FOLK 127A.

Phase 1 · Discover

This phase is about getting to know each other and the process: how a rebrand actually happens. Research comes first: mission, competition, mind-mapping, before any sketching starts. Students narrow to one business, refine their chosen mark, and start documenting their process from day one.

1Mon Aug 24 Complete
  • Introductions
  • Course Overview & Expectations
  • Mark Comparisons, assigned
  • Personal Identity, assigned
  • Process Book, assigned
2Mon Aug 31
Wed Sep 2
  • Mark Comparisons due Aug 31, 5%
  • Mark Comparisons crit: each student walks the class through all 5 marks, group discussion after each one
  • Quick-scan lecture and research on all 5 businesses: what they sell, who it's for, a competitor glance, what sets them apart
  • Vibe exploration on all 5: sketching, playing with typefaces, no narrowing yet
  • Continue exploring all 5 independently through the following week
3Wed Sep 9
  • No class Monday, Sep 7 (Labor Day, university closed)
  • Share-out: each student talks through what they've discovered and where they're leaning, across all 5
  • Narrow from 5 to 2, live in class
  • Lecture: file prep and production literacy (best practice, ungraded)
4Mon Sep 14
Wed Sep 16
  • High-contrast black and white roughs: refine sketches into bold, high-contrast drawings
  • Critique, then narrow to ONE business and mark direction
  • High-Contrast Drawings due Sep 16, 5%
5Mon Sep 21
Wed Sep 23
  • Dedicated refinement time: push the selected mark further before moving into system definition
  • Lecture: where CorpID work sits in the business world (brand strategy, marketing execution, the designer's role)

Phase 2 · Define

This is where we design the system for the brand: style-scape exploration first, then translating that direction into a locked system, color, type, grid, and the rationale behind it.

6Mon Sep 28
Wed Sep 30
  • Style-scape exploration: fast, loose, applied to a couple of sample contexts
  • Narrow to one direction, brief feedback round
7Mon Oct 5
Wed Oct 7
  • Lockup spec: logo lockup, clear space
  • Color palette, typography, and grid rules drafted
8Mon Oct 12
Wed Oct 14
  • Critique the system as a whole, written rationale drafted
  • System Definition due Oct 14, 10% (lockup spec, letterhead/stationery system, color/type/grid rules, written rationale)

Phase 4 · Deliver

By this phase, all elements of the brand system and applications should be complete and ready to go. This is where the semester turns to preparing for finals: presentation prep, process book, and final polish.

  • Process book: compile and review what you've been logging all semester, this is a check-in, not a start-from-scratch task
