Process Book

Weight: 10% Due: Wednesday, December 9

Your Process Book is the record of how you got here. Show the thinking behind your brand: the research, the sketches, the dead ends, the narrowing, and why you chose what you chose. It’s not a highlight reel.

Getting Started

  • You should already be building it.
    • If you’ve been saving your work as you go, this is mostly gathering it in order and labeling it.
    • If you haven’t, start now. Pull together everything you have since Mark Comparisons.
  • Show the process, not just the result.
    • Rejected directions, rough sketches, and things that didn’t work all belong here.
    • Evidence of thinking matters more than polish.
  • Label everything.
    • What it is, and a short note on why: what you learned, what you kept, what you dropped.
    • Note the crit feedback that changed your direction, and what you did with it.

What Goes In

In this order, following the semester.

  • Mark Comparisons.
  • Research.
    • Your business deep dive: purpose, customer, what makes it different.
    • Competitors and visual references.
    • This is where the full research behind your Brand Guide’s brand foundation lives.
  • Sketches. All of them, for both businesses.
  • High-Contrast Drawings.
  • Digitizing and refinement.
    • Iterations of your mark.
    • Narrowing from two businesses to one, and why.
  • System Definition.
    • Style-scapes, color, and type, including the directions you didn’t choose.
  • Applications development.
    • Early roughs, crit pin-ups, and revisions.
    • Show what changed after the November 9 and November 23 crits.
  • Where it landed. Keep it short. Your Brand Guide shows the finished system.
  • Personal Identity (optional). Its own section at the end, if you want to include it.
  • AI disclosure, if it applies. If you used AI for copy, note which pieces.

How It’s Different

  • Brand Guide: the finished rules, so someone else can use your brand.
  • Final Presentation: the pitch to your client.
  • Process Book: how you got there.

What to Submit in Brightspace

  • One PDF.
    • Everything in one file, in order.
    • There’s no page count. Complete and clear beats long or pretty.
    • Name it so I can tell what it is before I open it: your name and “Process Book.”
  • Due Wednesday, December 9.
