Assignments

Every graded component of the course, in one place. Due dates aren't locked in yet, once they are, a project timeline will go at the bottom of this page. Check Schedule for how these fit into the semester.

Graded Components

Mark Comparisons5%Discover
  • 10 mark comparisons across 5 business types: manufacturing, retail, professional, service, non-profit
  • 5 mark types per business: pictogram/icon, abstract symbol, monogram, logotype/wordmark, combination mark
  • Formatted as clean, consistent comparison pages
High-Contrast Drawings5%Discover
  • Refined concepts redrawn as bold, high-contrast black-and-white roughs
  • Reformatted into clean pages, consistent scale and organization
  • Critiqued in class before narrowing to one business
System Definition10%Define
  • Style-scape exploration and lockup definition, looped together until the system holds up
  • Final logo lockup spec, letterhead/stationery system, color palette, typography, and grid rules
  • Written rationale explaining the thinking behind the system
Personal Identity5%Self-paced
  • A personal identity system: typographic mark and business card
  • Introduced week 1, developed in parallel with the main project all semester
  • No fixed checkpoints, due near the end of the Develop phase
Brand Guide + Applications60%Develop → Finals
  • Finalized brand guidelines: logo system and clear space, variations and misuse examples, full color system, typography, usage standards
  • Minimum 10 applications: 6 required plus 4 free-choice, specific to the business (full breakdown below)
  • Free-choice applications go through explore-then-narrow: a wide set of quick directions first, then narrowed to the 4 built to portfolio quality
Process Book10%Deliver
  • Full documentation of the process: mark comparisons, concept sketches, high-contrast drawings, refinement and iteration, stationery development, application production
  • Evidence of thinking (sketches, dead ends, narrowing) matters more than a polished highlight reel
Final Presentation5%Finals
  • Live presentation to a panel of industry professionals
  • Covers company mission, design rationale, system overview, and application highlights
  • Professional dress, timed slot, deck submitted

Total: 100%

Required Applications

Part of Brand Guide + Applications, above. 6 fixed categories plus 4 free-choice applications specific to the business, 10 minimum total.

6 Required Website (home + interior, desktop and mobile) · Ad campaign (3 series of 3 ads) · Exterior signage · Invoice · App design (3–5 screens) · HTML email template + landing page
4 Free-Choice Chosen by the student, specific to their business. Explored broadly first (8–10 quick, low-fidelity directions), then narrowed to the 4 executed to portfolio quality.
