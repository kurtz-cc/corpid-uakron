High-Contrast Drawings

Phase: Discover Weight: 5% Due: Wednesday, September 23

Your rough sketches get narrowed down and redrawn clean. This is what you'll present at Critique Day, and it's the last stop before your concepts become digital logos.

Before This Is Due

  • Concept sketching.25 sketches for each of the 5 mark types, per business: 125 sketches per business, 250 total. Quantity over polish: fast, loose, exploratory. Every sketch, even the bad ones, goes in your process book.
  • Monday, Sep 21: Critique Day.Pin your sketches to the board. We'll critique as a class: what's working, what to refine, and you'll leave with one mark direction chosen per business.

The Assignment

  • One 17×11 sheet per business, two sheets total.Both businesses, not just one.
  • All 5 mark types on each sheet.Pictogram/Icon, Abstract Symbol, Monogram, Logotype/Wordmark, Combination Mark, each about 3×3 inches.
  • Clean, bold, black and white only.No gray. This is the refined redraw of your critique-day picks, not a new round of rough sketching.
  • Labeled by mark type.Make it easy to tell which drawing is which at a glance.

What's Next

  • Submit before class, Sep 23.Both sheets, both businesses.
  • Sep 23 class time: digitize.Turn both concepts into refined digital logos, ready by Sep 28.

Examples

Real examples from past students. Meant to show what a finished set of high-contrast drawings can look like, not templates to copy.
