Personal Identity

Weight: 5% Due: Wednesday, November 18

You are your first client. Every portfolio review, job application, and interview starts with your name on something you designed. Use the same thinking as your main project, at a smaller scale.

Getting Started

  • Ask the same questions you asked for your business.
    • Who is it for: studios, employers, clients?
    • What should they think of you?
    • What makes you different?
  • Work on it alongside your applications.
    • It’s a good project to switch to when you’re stuck.
    • A little at a time, starting now. Don’t save it for the week of November 16.

What to Make

  • A typographic mark.
    • Built from your name or initials.
    • Works in one color.
    • Holds up small, like an avatar or favicon.
    • A full version and a simplified version.
  • A business card.
    • Front and back.
    • Real information: your name, email, and portfolio.
    • Same type and color as your mark.
  • A resume.
    • One page.
    • Built on a grid. Show me the grid.
    • Real content, no placeholder text.
    • Same system as your mark and card.

Optional

Not required. Worth doing if you’ll actually use it.

  • Cover letter or letterhead
  • Email signature
  • Portfolio site header
  • Social avatar

What to Submit in Brightspace

  • One PDF.
    • Your mark, full and simplified.
    • Business card, front and back.
    • Resume.
    • Any optional pieces you made.
  • Flat files are fine.No mockups needed.
  • Due Wednesday, November 18.
