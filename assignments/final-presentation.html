Final Presentation

Weight: 5% Presenting: Wednesday, December 9 PDF due: Tuesday, December 8, 11:59pm

Pretend the panel is your client. Frame the challenge their business faces, then show why your system is the solution. This is a pitch, not a flip-through of your Brand Guide.

The Night Of

  • Wednesday, December 9, 5:15 to 8:00pm.FOLK 127A.
  • About 5 minutes to present, plus 3 for the judges’ questions.A timer will be on screen.
  • Every deck runs from my laptop.No switching computers, so your PDF has to be in Brightspace the night before.
  • Stay for the whole session.
  • Dress like you’re meeting a client.

Who’s Judging

  • A panel of three industry professionals.They score you while you present.
  • What they score.
    • Concept & Strategy
    • Mark Design
    • System Cohesion
    • Application Quality
    • Craftsmanship
    • Presentation & Communication
    • Overall Impression
  • Winners are announced that night.First place gets a prize.

Tell the Story in Order

  • The client’s challenge.About 30 seconds, 1–2 slides.
  • The original logo, and why it needed a redesign.About 30 seconds, 1–2 slides.
  • The business, and the one insight that shaped your direction.About 45 seconds, 1–2 slides.
  • The final mark and system.About 1:30, 3–5 slides.
  • Application highlights.About 1:45, 5–6 slides.

About 15 slides total, roughly 20 seconds each. Most of your time goes to the final work. Research and sketches belong in your Process Book, not here.

Open Strong, Close the Loop

  • Open with the client’s problem, not “Hi, my project is…”
  • End by tying your system back to that problem.
  • Rehearse it out loud, with a timer.
  • Expect “why” questions. Have your reasons ready.

How to Show It

  • Build it in your own deck template.The template is one of your applications. This presentation is its first real use.
  • Mockups, not process clutter.
  • A few things per slide.Use more slides instead.
  • Give every slide time,chapter slides too.

Timeline

  • Wed, Dec 2: Draft review.Bring every slide, in order. Polish comes after.
  • Tue, Dec 8, 11:59pm: PDF due in Brightspace.
  • Wed, Dec 9: Present.

What to Submit in Brightspace

  • One PDF of your presentation,in the Final Presentation dropbox, by Tuesday, December 8 at 11:59pm.
  • The file you submit is the file I’ll run.No updates after the deadline.
