Attendance & Late Work

The specifics students actually need, in plain language. The syllabus links out to the official University policy pages, this page spells out what that means in practice for this class.

Attendance

This is the official Myers School of Art Attendance Policy, applied consistently across all Myers courses, not something specific to this class. Full text lives at uakron.edu/art/academics/resources.dot.

How it works You can miss up to one week of class (2 sessions, for our M/W schedule) with no penalty. Starting with the next absence after that, your final course grade drops by one partial step for each additional absence: A → A- → B+ → B → B-, and so on. It is not a full letter per absence.
Late arrivals and early departures Three late arrivals or early departures count as one absence. Late arrival means entering after attendance has been taken, it is your responsibility to let me know you arrived late, otherwise it is marked as an absence.
Extenuating circumstances If something comes up, contact me before class starts whenever possible. You do not need to share details, just let me know you will not be there.

Late Work

This part is course-specific, set by me, not a University-wide policy.

Penalty Each class period a project is late results in a one letter-grade reduction on that project.
Cutoff Work more than one week late will not be accepted and receives an F for that project.

Questions about either policy: talk to me before it becomes a problem, not after.
